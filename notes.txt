           PROMPT:
           Using Pyfluidsynth to control fluidsynth on Raspberry Pi, is it possible to have instument on midi channel 1 send output to one effects bus and have another instrument on midi channel 2 send to a different effects bus. Effects bus in this case is an external LV2 effect that is connected with jack and pipewire. 

           ANSWER (partial):
           FluidSynth natively supports multi-channel audio rendering, meaning you can split its audio output into separate stereo pairs per MIDI channel. When using JACK (which integrates seamlessly with PipeWire on Raspberry Pi), FluidSynth exposes these distinct stereo pairs as individual output ports. You can then route each pair to a separate external LV2 effect plugin.
